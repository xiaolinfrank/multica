# Idea-generation frameworks

Open the technique that fits the stall, not all of them. Each entry says what it
is good for and what it produces, so choosing is one decision, not a survey.

## SCAMPER — remix something that already exists

Use when the subject is a product, process, or offer and the member wants
variants rather than a new concept.

| Letter | Question | Typical yield |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Substitute | What can we replace — material, rule, person, channel? | cheaper or compliant variant |
| Combine | What can we merge — two features, two audiences, two steps? | bundle / one-stop offer |
| Adapt | What can we borrow from another industry? | proven pattern, new context |
| Modify / magnify / minify | What if we doubled or halved one dimension? | premium tier, micro version |
| Put to another use | Who else could use this unchanged? | new segment, no build cost |
| Eliminate | What can we remove entirely? | simplification that reads as innovation |
| Reverse | What if we did the opposite, or ran the order backwards? | business-model flip, self-service |

## Six thinking hats — force the missing perspective

Use when a group (or the member) is stuck in one register — usually all
optimism or all risk.

| Hat | Only allowed to talk about | Use it to |
| --- | --- | --- |
| White | facts, data, what is known and missing | surface the evidence gap |
| Red | feelings, hunches, no justification needed | catch the objection nobody will argue |
| Black | risks, why it fails | pre-mortem, cheapest value in the set |
| Yellow | benefits, best case | recover upside after a black-hat pass |
| Green | new options, provocations | generate, no judging |
| Blue | process, what we are doing now | stop the meeting from spiralling |

Run them in sequence, each hat for a bounded stretch, and record which hat said
what — mixing them is how a decision gets made twice.

## How might we — turn a complaint into an opening

Rewrite the problem as a question that presumes a solution exists:

- Complaint: "医生不看我们的提醒。" → HMW: "我们怎样才能让提醒在医生做决定的那一秒出现？"
- Complaint: "报表没人填。" → HMW: "我们怎样才能让填表这件事本身就有用？"

A good HMW is narrow enough to act on and wide enough not to contain the answer.
If the HMW already names the solution ("how might we build a dashboard"), you
have smuggled the answer in — widen it.

## Inversion — guarantee the failure, then reverse it

Ask: **what would make this fail as fast as possible?** List 10 ways. Each one,
reversed, is a design requirement — and the list doubles as the risk register.
Works when the member is anchored on a plan and cannot see its downside.

## 100 → 10 → 3 (Jeffrey Emanuel's filter)

Use when the option space is genuinely open and the member wants a defensible
shortlist rather than a favourite.

1. **100**: quantity with no filter, on distinct axes.
2. **10**: rank against four lens — is the concept sound, does it solve a real
   need, can it be executed, is the debt worth it.
3. **3**: re-challenge the ten ("what is weak, what is missing, what is the
   better version"), then keep three. If all three share an assumption, generate
   three answers to that assumption instead.

## Analogy transfer — steal the structure, not the surface

Fill this row and read across: who else has the same **structural** problem?

| Our problem | Structurally identical in | What they do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| onboarding new agents | hospital shift handover | standardised handoff note |
| pricing a scarce expert | airline yield management | tiered, time-based price |
| review bottleneck | code review queues | small batches, owner rotation |

Ignore surface differences (industry, vocabulary). Lift the mechanism.

## Constraint removal — one constraint at a time

"If money were not an issue…", "if this shipped tomorrow…", "if we could not use
software…". Then ask what part of the unconstrained answer survives. That
residue is usually the real idea; the rest was scaffolding.

## When to stop diverging

Stop when a second batch produces mostly reshuffles of the first. Continuing past
that point produces volume without information — go converge, and let the reject
ledger carry the leftovers.
